1.9 OPTIMIZING DATABASE:(using set,arithmetic,logical and special operators).

1. Display customer-id,city who lives in 'Kottayam' or 'EKM'.
mysql> SELECT id, city
    -> FROM customer
    -> WHERE city IN ('Kottayam', 'EKM');
+-----+----------+
| id  | city     |
+-----+----------+
| 666 | Kottayam |
+-----+----------+

2. Display product details with an update price amount.(new price=old price+5rs)
mysql> select pid,pname,price,price+5 as new_price from product;
+-----+--------+-------+-----------+
| pid | pname  | price | new_price |

|  11 | Pen    | 10.20 |     15.20 |
|  22 | Book   | 51.00 |     56.00 |
|  33 | Pencil | 25.50 |     30.50 |

3. Display customer details who doesn't have phone number and lives in 'Calicut' city(use logical operator).
mysql> select * from customer where ph_no is null and city='calicut';

4. List the product names if it find any records in the invoice_item table has quantity=3(use special operator).
mysql> select pname from product p where exists(select * from invoice_item i where i.pid=p.pid and i.quantity=3);
